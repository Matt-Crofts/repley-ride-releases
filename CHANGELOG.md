What changed in REPley Ride, the app on your computer, newest first.

## Next

- **ANT+ trainers and straps** through a USB stick, on Linux: REPley Ride picks the radio that reaches your trainer,
  Bluetooth first, and Advanced on Your bike lets you choose. Your trainer and strap show which radio they are on.
- **Start is instant.** Your trainer connects while you choose a ride and is held ready, so Start is just the
  countdown, and the ride's clock starts with your first pedal stroke.
- **Help in one line.** When something is in the way of a good connection, the start screen says what, and offers
  the one button that fixes it.
- **Clearer when a trainer will not take control:** it says whether to update the trainer's firmware, in its
  maker's app, or to remove an old pairing in Windows' Bluetooth settings.
- **Look for it now** in the ride's menu finds a dropped trainer at once. The ride never stops for it.
- **Updates in one click:** REPley Ride downloads the new version itself and opens the installer.

## 0.5.4

- Windows' installer is signed by Chain Brothers Pty Ltd, so Windows names the publisher.
- Your trainer is held ready on the start screen, so Start waits for nothing.
- Each ride's details and its finish picture go to REPley, and your earlier rides once.
- Riders on the road only step aside to pass you; ride charts show your power as recorded; zone rides from your plan
  are held in ERG; and your rides are listed on Today.

## 0.5.3

- Follow along with your gym days on screen, beside your plan's rides.
- One way on at a time: one Start on Today, and nothing greyed out without a reason.
- Other riders on the road at your pace; ski lifts, creeks, snow and granite on the climbs; menus that fit a TV; a
  map you can zoom; and panels and the window remembered for each screen.
- Any of your own heart-rate straps connects, whichever one you are wearing.
