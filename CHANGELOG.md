## 0.17.1

- **Arches at every line:** every sprint, KOM and finish line has an inflatable arch in place of the old metal gantry. Sprint arches are green, KOM arches white with red polka dots, and each reads START or FINISH.
- **Segments read one way:** a segment's arch and road paint face the riders it counts for, with chevrons at the start. Ridden the other way, you see plain REPley Ride branding.
- **A loop at the top of each climb:** climbs now turn round a road loop about a landscaped island, three times the old roundabout's size. Each turn adds about 90 m, and you ease into it instead of braking at once.
- **Bigger turnarounds:** Beach Road's roundabouts are bigger, every turnaround has a TURNAROUND arch with the crowd behind barriers, and no footpath runs into them. Other riders now go round instead of turning on the spot.
- **Real coaches and a TV truck:** the team coach, the WOMBAT coach and the TV truck are proper vehicles, with windows, doors, mirrors, lights and the dish on the truck.
- **Better buildings:** distant buildings, and every building on a phone, have framed windows, doors, alpine shutters and shopfronts under verandahs. Many Australian houses gain a front verandah.
- **More detailed parked cars:** door lines, handles, number plates and spoked wheels on the higher settings. Phones keep the simpler cars.
- **Bike lanes:** the painted symbols show a whole bicycle, and the green paint where side streets cross the lane shows the asphalt through it.
- **Café Racer:** the bikes and diners outside the café sit on the footpath, not sunk into it.
- **Smoother creeks:** streams beside the climbs curve between rounded banks and flow downstream. Where a creek meets the road, it runs underneath.
- **See your rider on You:** You shows your rider on the bike in your kit and gear, with Edit rider one press away.
- **Light, dark or match your system:** choose the look of the pages on You. Match system follows your computer or phone, and the window opens in the colour you chose.
- **The steps before you start:** Workouts, Build your own and Your program list a workout's steps, from the warm-up through each set and its rest to the cool-down, with the watts.
- **Devices holds your trainer:** Edit rider is now Rider, Graphics and Account. Your trainer and strap live in Devices, top right of every page, and a dot there says when something needs a look.
- **A quicker first run:** setting up now asks only what sizes your rides. Settings such as how power is shown wait in Edit rider.
- **You fits a phone:** its buttons sit in one row of icons at the foot, so they no longer cover your rides.
- **Your look on a phone:** the locker's slots are a row you swipe, with bigger tiles and one line each.
- **The road to the top, first:** on a climb the big number is now the distance left to the top, in metres once you are close. The metres still to climb sit beside it.

## 0.17.0

- **Shadows to the horizon:** on High and Ultra, buildings and trees cast shadows well beyond the road around you, and corners and doorways sit in soft shade.
- **Late afternoon by default:** rides start in a low, warm sun against a cool sky. Pick another time of day from the button beside the route before a ride.
- **Clouds that look like clouds:** ragged, uneven cumulus that take on the sun's colour at dusk.
- **Haze and colour:** a light haze softens the far suburbs, and a gentle colour grade warms the whole scene.
- **Street lamps at dusk:** lamp heads glow and throw warm pools of light on the road as the sun goes down.
- **Gum trees:** trunks taper gradually and branches keep their thickness, as real gums do.
- **Standing to accelerate:** your rider stands when you push harder and sits again once the power settles.
- **Pausing on a hill:** your rider now stops within seconds when you pause, even on a steep descent. Only the picture changes, not your trainer.
- **Helmets that fit:** every helmet sits snugly on the head with no bulges at the ears, and the female rider's head has a natural shape.
- **Natural hair:** short hair looks cropped, a ponytail grows out of the head, and the hairline is clean round the ear.
- **Beards and moustaches:** choose stubble or a short beard on the Rider tab. Earn a moustache, a goatee and a full beard with badges.
- **Hair styles to earn:** the swept quiff comes with the Summit badge and the long plait with Park pass. A style you already wear stays yours.
- **Faces:** the male rider's face has more natural proportions, and chins no longer stretch when the head lifts on the bike.
- **Your own intervals:** on Workouts, Build your own sets how many efforts, how long and how hard, the rest between, and the warm-up and cool-down.
- **A ramp test runs every step:** steps can't be skipped or eased. A pause ends the test without saving it, so a rest can't lift your FTP.
- **A fairer FTP from the ramp test:** only the minutes of the test itself count. Riding on after it ends, up a climb or in a sprint, no longer changes the result.
- **Weight in your units:** the ramp test result shows your watts per kilo with your weight in kg, pounds, or stone and pounds, as you chose.
- **Faces that move:** riders blink and breathe, smile a little when the riding is easy, and breathe through an open mouth, squint and frown when they push hard.
- **Your effort on your face:** your rider's expression follows your watts against your FTP, so a hard interval shows.
- **Grounded bikes:** a soft shadow sits under every bike, darkest under the tyres, so riders look planted on the road in shade and on overcast days too.
- **Bike lettering:** every bike carries REPley on both sides of the down tube, in your kit's accent colour.
- **Melbourne terraces:** in Albert Park, Middle Park and St Kilda, terrace rows have parapets, wing walls and iron verandahs with cast-iron lace.
- **Thirties flats:** some St Kilda and Elwood flats are cream render with a band at every floor and a stepped front over the entrance.
- **Fern forest:** on the 1 in 20, Donna Buang and Mount Buller, ground ferns now fill the forest floor beside the road.
- **Timing gantries:** a gantry stands over each sprint, KOM and finish line, and its banner matches the line painted on the road.
- **Four places instead of seven:** the menu is now Your program, Workouts, Ride and You, on a computer and on a phone's tab bar.
- **Ride holds Free ride, Pace partner and the Adventure park:** three tabs with an icon each, and Ride opens on the one you rode last.
- **The park is easy to find:** before it opens, its tab shows how many of its three steps you have done. A tap shows what is left, with a way to ride each step.
- **The park says when it opens:** its tab glows and reads New, Ride gets a dot and a banner appears, until you visit it once. The ride that earns it has Go to the park.
- **Devices from any page:** a Devices button sits top right of every page and opens your trainer and strap without leaving where you are. A preview never needs one.
- **Workouts start from what you want:** pick Ride longer, Raise my FTP, Top-end fitness, Sprint power, Recover or Test my FTP. Then pick your time, and the three best matches show.
- **Short on time:** choose 30 or 45 minutes and longer sessions are cut to fit at the same targets. Each one says how much of the work it keeps.
- **Build your own stands apart:** a dashed tile marked Custom opens its own page, with the chart of your set as you build it.
- **All-out sprints are all out:** during a sprint the trainer lets go of ERG, so the road and your gear set the resistance, and ERG comes back for the recovery. You can hold sprints in ERG instead.
- **Two sprint sessions from studies:** Short sprints 3 × 4 × 6 s and Sprints 20 × 6 s, each as its study rode it.
- **Build your own goes all out:** one press of All out makes every effort a sprint, and efforts now go down to five seconds.
- **Your program fits your time:** today's session offers 30 min, 45 min or As planned. A shorter version counts as today, done shorter.
- **Rides that do the work count:** a ride from another page that does today's work counts for today. The summary says so, with Undo.
- **One look for every page:** a plain title, one choice, and Ready to ride at the foot with the preview and Start.
- **A light theme:** turn it on in You, Edit rider, Graphics, Pages. The ride itself and pictures with words on them stay dark.
- **See who you ride with:** Pace partner shows the robot you picked riding on Beach Road, alone or as a bunch of five, in its pace band's colour.

## 0.16.0

- **One HUD button for the whole screen:** HUD on the ride's bar opens one panel with four layouts (Full, Simple, Clean and Off) and a switch for each part. D goes round the four.
- **Intervals at a glance:** the workout's steps sit in a card at the left, and the one you're on fills in its zone's colour as you ride it. Compact shows the next two, List the next four.
- **Your workout along the bottom:** with the profile on, the whole workout runs across the foot of the screen with a line where you are.
- **Glass panels:** the map, the climb's chart, the intervals and the banners let the road show through, blurred. On Low graphics they keep a plain tint for a smoother ride.
- **Nothing covers anything:** the banners sit under the numbers whatever their size, and the course's name stops short of your power.
- **A tidier menu:** the map, the segments and the workout's view moved from the menu to the HUD panel.
- **Your workout, step by step, after the ride:** the summary shows each step in its zone's colour, held, missed, skipped, stopped part way or ridden with ERG off a while, and the steps you didn't reach in one line.
- **Junctions that look like junctions:** each road meets the junction at its own mouth, and the kerb and verge turn the corner on a curve. The round patch of tarmac with kerbs cut off inside it is gone, at Albert Park's start too.
- **Kerbs on both sides of the road:** on Beach Road's streets one side's kerb, gutter and grass strip, and some footpaths, were never drawn. Both sides have them now.
- **Roads that end square at a junction:** a road's lines stop straight across where the junction begins, and no grass shows through its tarmac.
- **No loose gravel on the Grand Prix circuit:** the gravel beside Albert Park's bends lay about the grass in separate slabs. Only the tightest bends have it now, in one piece that narrows at its ends.
- **Close the app on Android:** a Close REPley Ride button sits at the foot of the menu, and a Close button on the ride-saved screen sends your ride first. It needs the next Android build, not a live update.
- **Phone reports reach the beta:** your rides and the "Send a setup report" button now report from the Android and iPhone apps, the same as on a computer. Reports made offline wait and go at your next launch.
- **Share your ride's pictures on Android:** "Share the pictures" stopped with an error, because Android wouldn't hand the app's own pictures to other apps. Your screenshots and the ride card now open in the share sheet, ready for Strava. It needs the next Android build, not a live update.
- **Rides reach Strava from the Android app:** the phone sent the ride file as garbled text, so REPley turned it away. It now arrives whole, and if REPley ever refuses a file again the ride says why.
- **Your FTP on every device:** signed in, your FTP, weight, kit and settings follow you, so an FTP raised on your phone sizes the computer's next ride. FTP and weight go only with your OK to share body data.
- **A new device starts where you left off:** sign in on a new computer and your FTP, weight and kit are there, ready to ride.
- **Each screen keeps its own layout:** a TV can stay on Full while your phone keeps Simple.
- **Answer once about your body data:** whether heart rate, power and weight go with your rides is one answer for you, on every device. Say no on your phone and your computer stops sending them too.
- **Smoother rides on Android phones:** Melbourne and the climbs run at about 60 frames a second on a mid-range phone, up from as low as 15, with a sharper picture. A warm phone draws a little softer instead of stuttering.
- **Your ride report says how smoothly it ran:** it now includes your device's graphics chip and frame rate, so a slow phone shows up without you doing a thing.
- **A fuller Beach Road:** more houses and blocks of flats line the route, and the beach side stays open as it really is.
- **Café Racer Old School:** the cyclists' café stands on its Marine Parade corner, with tables and bikes out front.
- **Trees that look like trees:** plane trees, elms, figs and gums line Melbourne's roads, each with its own bark, and Norfolk Island pines stand on the Beach Road foreshore. The Stelvio has spruce, fir and pine.
- **Windows with rooms behind them:** houses and shops catch the sky and show curtains, blinds and lit shelves as you ride past.
- **A street that's lived in:** cars parked down the side streets, road bikes leaning on racks, and café tables under umbrellas with people at them.
- **Grass you can see:** real grass on the nature strips beside the road, on High and Ultra.
- **Front gardens:** leafy hedges and shrubs instead of green boxes.
- **Side streets that join the road:** every side street off Beach Road now meets it on curved kerbs, with the bike lane painted green across the corner.
- **Footpaths the whole way:** the footpath runs along Beach Road's houses without gaps, turns each corner and crosses every side street.
- **Suburbs filled in:** streets behind the beach have houses behind houses now, and Brighton has homes on both sides of the road.
- **A start and finish arch:** an inflatable REPley Ride arch marks the start of every ride and the finish line.
- **Out of the saddle:** your rider stands up to push hard on steep climbs and in sprints, and other riders do too.
- **Lifesaving clubs:** each club along the bay has its building and red and yellow patrol flags on the sand.
- **Smoother on phones:** Luna Park and the St Kilda end ride several frames a second faster.
- **Your strap before you ride:** the start card says when no heart-rate strap is chosen, with Find my strap right there, and a ride without one asks once, a minute in. A Garmin or Apple watch shows up once its heart-rate broadcast is on.
- **Strava from the start:** setting up asks whether your rides go to Strava, and once it is connected you can send the rides of the last month too.
- **Fewer screenshots:** a ride takes at most four of its own, two minutes apart, so the ones it takes are the ones worth keeping.
- **Pictures in your photos:** on a phone, screenshots land in your photos in a REPley Ride album, and the ride's card shares straight from the summary.
- **Your rides, and progress with your program:** the Journey page is now Your rides, every ride you have done. Your level, this week, your FTP and badges sit at the top of Your program, with See all progress for the rest.

## 0.15.0

- **How did it feel, in one tap:** after a ride, tap Easy, Moderate, Hard, Very hard or All out. It keeps your progression accurate toward your goal, and your coach sees it too.
- **Every interval checked:** each interval of tempo or harder shows as held or missed as you ride, and the summary lists them. Say why you missed one, so a busy day never counts against you.
- **Your FTP from your rides:** the Journey offers an FTP worked out from six weeks of your indoor rides, one tap to use, beside your rider profile. You need to be signed in to REPley.
- **A ramp test sets your FTP straight away:** with one tap to keep your old one. A strong ride now offers its higher FTP rather than changing it for you.
- **Workouts your way on screen:** pick Full, Compact or Off for the workout card from the menu or with W. Skip sits on the card, and the countdown to the next interval always shows.
- **A calmer screen:** the bottom bar fades after a few seconds. Segment banners shrink once you've read them, and a junction's choices tuck away once you've picked.
- **Gates you pass once:** an interval gate on a U-turn, like the one at Station Pier, no longer counts you through it twice.
- **Rides that start without your strap:** if your heart-rate strap or cadence sensor needs an ANT+ stick that isn't plugged in, the ride starts anyway and tells you, instead of refusing to start.
- **Watch your world being built:** the loading card is smaller so you can see the world take shape, and the course you're likely to ride is built ahead on the start screen.
- **Lighter rain:** rain on Low draws faster, for a smoother ride on modest graphics.
- **A new look:** REPley Ride's icon is REPley's barbell with chainring plates and a crankset's spider, centred.

## 0.14.2

- **Legs that turn on every trainer:** if your trainer sends power but no cadence, your rider pedals at a rate that fits your power. The ride offers to add a Bluetooth or ANT+ cadence sensor, once, and remembers if you say no.
- **Legs that stay whole at the hips:** the rider's thighs and bib shorts no longer tear or bulge at the buttocks at some points of the pedal stroke. The hips now turn smoothly through the whole circle.
- **A start card that tells the truth:** a Melbourne ride now names its area, and shows one lap's distance and climbing (a ride with no lap, like Beach Road, says "Ride as far as you like"). Before, it showed the length of road built ahead and called it a made-up climb.
- **A smoother profile:** the course profile on the start card is a gentle curve, and a flat road no longer counts small bumps in the data as climbing.
- **Simulator rides skip the strap search:** the start card says it is loading the course, not connecting, and no heart-rate strap is looked for.
- **Melbourne loads about a third faster:** the Port Melbourne loop builds in 15 seconds here instead of 23, and Ultra in 18 instead of 27.
- **A road helmet that looks like one:** the helmet sits lower and longer, with deep vents between solid ridges and dark foam showing through them. Skin shows fine pores and shoes a leather grain in close-ups, and the women's hair is drawn back off the face.
- **A note when a sign-in ends:** the app now keeps a short log of when your REPley sign-in was renewed or ended, and why, in a session-log.txt beside your account file. It never holds your password or sign-in code.

## 0.14.1

- **Steadier power on screen:** choose each second, a 3 s or a 5 s average under Rider. New riders start on 3 s. Recording and uploads keep every second.
- **Melbourne's roads at surveyed heights:** Beach Road and nearby roads now follow surveyed heights. The false 12% blips at Black Rock are gone and the real pinches stay. Against real Strava rides, grade is within 0.5% over any 500 m.
- **Your partner's pace on its back:** a pace partner shows the W/kg it holds, steady, up hills and down, the same as its tag.
- **Robots in bib shorts:** the pace robots' hips are graphite like cycling shorts, and their heads a little smaller.
- **Willunga Hill from LiDAR:** its road now follows South Australia's 2022 laser survey, accurate to a few centimetres. It measures 3.04 km at 7.4% with 224 m of climbing, matching the published figures.

- **The vented helmet for everyone:** everyone starts in the vented road helmet. The aero helmet (1,000 km), aero socks (500 km) and Everest suit are earned, saving a few watts to keep racing fair.

- **A tidier locker:** gear is grouped by rarity, commons first.

- **Your partner's watts on its back:** a pace partner shows its live watts under the REPley lettering, where you see it from its wheel.
- **Pace bands at a glance:** a pace partner's goggle frame (Cranko) or visor rim (Gearia) wears its band's colour, the same as its tag.
- **A friendlier female rider:** her face is new, softer and smiling.

- **Choose your way home:** at St Kilda beach and St Kilda Pier, pick the foreshore to Station Pier, out along the pier, or on along Beach Road. At Station Pier the road just carries on.
- **No more green islands in the bay:** small mounds of grass that stood out of the water 200 m off the shore are gone.

## 0.12.0

- **Trainer search ignores other devices:** the Wi-Fi and wired trainer search no longer lists an Apple TV, speaker or printer on your network. Nothing about those devices is kept or logged.
- **See what you've done:** on Your program a finished day turns green with a tick and shows your time, distance and average power, or your sets. Each week shows as a ring.
- **Every day in the program strip:** the strip at the top of each ride page now shows every day, ticked when done, instead of only the gym days and extra rides.
- **Junctions you can read from the sofa:** the ways out sit left to right as the roads go, so ← and → (or the D-pad) move between them. Your way is teal with a tick.
- **See your controller connect:** plug in or switch on a controller mid-ride and the app says so for 8 seconds, with what its buttons do. A mark on Devices shows while it's connected.
- **Melbourne rides never freeze:** on a long ride the picture used to stop while the clock and distance went on. The world now follows you however far you ride.
- **Lighter long rides in Melbourne:** the ride no longer builds road thousands of kilometres ahead as it goes, which slowed the app the longer you rode.
- **St Kilda is a simple fork:** Beach Road and Beaconsfield Parade run straight through the junction and Fitzroy Street turns off to Albert Park. No more detour to a roundabout and back.
- **Ride on to Port Melbourne:** Beaconsfield Parade now runs 4 km along the bay to Station Pier, past the beach, palms and city skyline. Ride it as its own course or straight on from Beach Road.
- **Smoother turns at every Melbourne junction:** whichever way you go through a junction, you ride one smooth curve on to the next road, with no jump or wobble on the way.
- **Out of St Kilda the way a rider goes:** Beach Road heads straight down Jacka Boulevard under the tram on The Esplanade, no doubling back. It no longer circles the Black Rock clock tower.
- **Room to pass:** other riders coming past step aside from where you are on the road, so shoulders and arms no longer overlap.
- **A steadier rider's-eye view:** the head rocks a third as much as you pedal, so climbing slowly the road no longer bumps with every stroke.
- **The map's arrow turns with you:** after a U-turn, and riding back along Beach Road, the arrow points the way you're going instead of backwards.
- **A U-turn on a turning loop does nothing:** at the end of a road, where the loop is already turning you round, U-turn no longer sends you riding backwards.
- **Wi-Fi and wired trainers without Bluetooth:** a Direct Connect trainer on Wi-Fi or Ethernet works on a computer with no Bluetooth, or with it off. The app says Bluetooth is missing and keeps looking.
- **Network, not Bluetooth:** a Direct Connect trainer shows as Network (Wi-Fi or Ethernet) with its own mark, and switching back from ANT+ says "Back on Network".
- **Add a trainer by its address:** if a Wi-Fi or wired trainer doesn't show up (guest Wi-Fi and some routers hide it), add it by IP address. It's remembered next time.

## 0.11.0

- **Your program:** Today is now Your program. Every ride page opens with a strip showing your week, the days done, your next session and your goal, one press from riding it.
- **Swap a session for a similar one:** swap the day's workout for one of the same type and effort, often shorter. It still counts for the day, and your plan stays the same.
- **Extra rides count where you look:** a ride on top of your plan shows on Your program beside the week's days, in its own colour, and the strip counts it.
- **Program complete:** when the last week is ridden, Your program says so and offers your next block, or a new goal.
- **Every day rides indoors:** a day planned as an outdoor ride says so and offers Ride it indoors, which still counts it done.
- **Choosing a ride is simpler:** Melbourne has its own place on the Free ride page, with Beach Road and the Albert Park circuit as separate looping courses. Steer off at any junction with the arrow keys.
- **Where you ride, the same everywhere:** Your program, Workouts and Pace partner choose the course in the same dialog, now tall enough to show the courses in a grid.
- **Where you ride in groups:** the course dialog's All shows Melbourne, Climbs, Chill rides and Your routes under their own headings.
- **Follow a study:** the studied interval protocols as blocks of sessions in the study's order and dose, with where you are and your heart rate in the hard parts session by session.
- **The trainer keeps going when Bluetooth drops:** after 5 seconds without a link while you pedal, the app switches to the other radio (Bluetooth or ANT+) without stopping, and switches back when it can.
- **Speed, average speed and cadence:** power, heart rate and speed are the big numbers now, with average speed under speed. The gradient pops up by itself on a hill steeper than 3%.
- **Descents feel like Zwift's:** a descent goes to the trainer at half its grade before the road feel, so 100% is never a freewheel. Climbs are unchanged.
- **A smaller bar and menu:** the bar stays on one row on any screen, with a HUD button for the numbers, Finish asks above it, and the menu has only what the bar doesn't.
- **The keys work with a video open:** C, M and the other keys reach the ride while YouTube is open, unless you're typing in it. P takes a screenshot.
- **Signed in once on Linux:** on Omarchy and other Hyprland or Sway desktops the app keeps your sign-in in the keyring, so it no longer asks for a code every launch.
- **Melbourne fixes:** wheels sit on the road at junctions, kookaburras perch on lamps and bus shelters instead of mid-air, benches are proper slatted ones, and the Albert Park lake shore is smooth.
- **Finish is never under the climb profile:** on a smaller screen the ride bar shrinks step by step, from words to icons, so Pause and Finish can always be pressed.
- **Melbourne rides keep their name:** the summary, the share picture and Your rides say Beach Road or Albert Park circuit, not Mountain climb.
- **One button for the sun and the sky:** the sun follows your clock (sunrise early, golden hour from 4 pm) and the sky changes each ride unless you pick one.
- **Fits in and New to me:** one filter for the time you have, a tick on every course you have ridden, and New to me to hide them.
